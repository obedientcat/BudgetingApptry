This page presents proposed advanced features of iBudget intended for school administrators and future researchers. No real data is collected or analyzed in the current prototype.

For School Administrators

  • Identification of common financial challenges among working students
  • Overview of frequently reported expense categories
  • Conceptual trends for student financial well-being
  • Basis for planning student support and financial literacy programs

For Future Researchers

  • Framework for future longitudinal financial studies
  • Potential analysis of financial stress and well-being
  • Reference model for future budgeting platforms
  • Support for mixed-method research approaches

Ethical and Privacy Considerations

All proposed analytics are designed to utilize anonymized and aggregated data only. No personally identifiable information shall be collected without proper consent.
